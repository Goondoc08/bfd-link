Tips & Known Limits

Helpful tips

Install it to your home screen
Runs full screen with no address bar, and the app itself works with no signal. See Add to home screen at the bottom of the page.
Star what you use
Tap the ☆ on any tile to pin it to a Favorites row at the top. Saved on your phone only — everyone's is their own.
Tap the shift bar
The date bar at the top opens a month calendar showing the shift letter for every day.

Known limits

City Network tiles copy instead of opening
Those pages only exist inside the city network, and there's no station WiFi — they can't load on a phone on any connection. Tapping one copies the address so you can paste it on a city computer.
Store tiles land on the store page
They open the App Store / Play Store listing. Tap Open there to launch the app. A web page can't press that button for you.
Web Only tiles won't open the app
Pulsara opens the website even if you have the app installed. That's on Pulsara's end — no fix available.
Search only covers tile names
It matches link names and descriptions, not the text inside pages. No typo tolerance either.
Only the app works offline
The tiles and calendar stay available with no signal. The pages they link to still need a connection.
