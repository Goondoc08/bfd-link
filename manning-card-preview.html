Manning card — formatting round 2

Full unit names, red divider, row shading, tighter cards for non-split units, and the not-updated flag — all against today's real sheet.

Not-updated flag is new. Each unit's raw "Day 1/Day 2" text is still not shown (as agreed) — instead, its sheet value is compared against today's actual shift day (computed the same way the app's real shift rotation is, in the banner above). The gap this closes: the header always shows today's correct calendar day the instant it flips, but a unit's manning entry can still be showing yesterday's crew until someone updates it, typically not until ~0800. A stale entry shows Not updated so that's visible at a glance instead of silently looking current. A blank day field shows nothing — same as a real match — since there's nothing to compare a blank against, and it isn't evidence the manning itself is stale.
